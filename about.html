About the chapter

Building a stronger engineering community through IEEE.

The IEEE Student Chapter brings students together for technical learning, professional development, leadership opportunities, and service to the college community.

Our mission

Connecting knowledge with opportunity.

We support IEEE's mission by creating an inclusive environment where students can explore emerging technologies, strengthen practical skills, and learn from experienced professionals.

Through talks, seminars, technical activities, and peer collaboration, members gain confidence to contribute in the classroom, the workplace, and the wider engineering community.

Who we are

A student-led professional community.

As an official IEEE Student Chapter, we work under the guidance of our faculty mentor to deliver activities that reflect IEEE's commitment to technical excellence, ethical practice, and lifelong learning.

Our programme is led by students, supported by faculty, and strengthened by the members of our student committee.

Our programme

Four ways members get involved.

Focus 01Throughout the year

Technical learning

Focus 02Throughout the year

Professional development

Build communication, teamwork, and career skills through mentoring, industry interaction, and member-led sessions.

Focus 03Throughout the year

Leadership opportunities

Take responsibility for activities, coordinate teams, and gain practical experience leading initiatives from planning to delivery.

Focus 04Throughout the year

Outreach and service

Promote engineering education and support community initiatives that create a positive impact beyond the campus.
