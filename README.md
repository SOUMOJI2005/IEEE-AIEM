# IEEE Students Chapter Website

Welcome to the official website of our **IEEE Students Chapter**.

This website is designed to represent our student community, showcase our activities, promote technical events, and provide students with opportunities to learn, innovate, and collaborate.

 About IEEE Students Chapter

The IEEE Students Chapter is a student-driven technical community focused on developing knowledge and practical skills in areas such as **electronics, robotics, programming, automation, communication, and emerging technologies**.

Our goal is to create an environment where students can:

* Learn new technologies
* Participate in workshops and competitions
* Develop practical projects
* Improve technical and leadership skills
* Work together on innovative ideas
* Connect with other students and professionals

 Website Features

* Modern and responsive design
* IEEE Students Chapter introduction
* Events and workshop section
* Robotics and electronics activities
* Team/member information
* Project showcase
* Registration and contact section
* Interactive animations
* Mobile-friendly layout

 Technologies Used

The website is developed using:

* **HTML5** — Website structure
* **CSS3** — Styling, layout, and animations
* **JavaScript** — Interactive functionality
